# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and specific.
- Give feedback about the work and its user impact.
- Welcome questions from new embedded developers.
- Credit code, artwork, research and testing contributions.
- Respect privacy and coordinate security reports responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted disclosure of private information and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that do not follow this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports can be made privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile or the repository's private security-reporting channel.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
